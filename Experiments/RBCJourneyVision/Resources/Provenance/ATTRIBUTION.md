# Anatomy and vascular asset attribution

`brain_anatomy_realistic_v2.usdz`, related registered anatomy layers, and
`cranial_vascular_registered_assembly_v2.usdz` are derived from the
Z-Anatomy / BodyParts3D source recorded by the Stroke-VisionOS v2 manifests.

- Source: Z-Anatomy / BodyParts3D
- License: CC BY-SA 4.0 / CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan
- Modifications: selection, registration, material preparation, and USDZ export
  for generic spatial education
- Patient-specific: no
- Specialist review: required

The cerebral blood-flow, lumen, red-cell, and microcirculation assets are custom
conceptual educational geometry from the same Stroke-VisionOS asset kit. They
are not CFD, quantitative hemodynamics, or measured patient data.
