# Paper Shader notice

The calm environmental horizon reuses the Paper Shaders WebGL library from
<https://github.com/paper-design/shaders>. The bundled upstream library is
licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0; the complete licence is at
`vendor/paper-shaders/LICENSE`.

The Stroke Care wrapper, palette, motion settings, and medical-use boundaries
are local project work derived from the earlier Ashfall Vision experiment.
The shader is an atmospheric transition only. It is not anatomical geometry,
blood flow, perfusion, pressure, emotion sensing, or a patient measurement.
