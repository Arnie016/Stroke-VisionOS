Stroke Care — Third-Party Anatomy Notices
==========================================

This prototype includes atlas-derived teaching anatomy from Z-Anatomy and
BodyParts3D. The derived geometry remains subject to the upstream attribution
and ShareAlike terms:

- Z-Anatomy — Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
  https://www.z-anatomy.com/
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
- BodyParts3D — Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 2.1 Japan
  https://lifesciencedb.jp/bp3d/
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.1/jp/

The registered-v2 dural venous sinus and internal jugular reference is a
generic atlas-derived educational model. Purple/blue is a display convention;
it does not depict blood colour, oxygenation, flow direction, velocity,
pressure, stenosis, collateral status, perfusion, or a patient scan. The asset
must not be used for diagnosis, treatment planning, navigation, device sizing,
procedural rehearsal, or a clinical decision. Anatomical identity,
registration, terminology, and patient-facing use require specialist review.

Project asset manifest: vision_pro_stroke_kit_v2 /
asset_manifest_cranial_vascular_v2.json, version 0.4.0-cranial-vascular.

The Scholar Surface view includes an NIH Human Reference Atlas male scalp
cutaway and NIH Visible Human eye context, distributed under Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International:

- NIH 3D 3DPX-021016 v2 / HRA Skin, Male / CC BY 4.0
- NIH 3D 3DPX-020591 v1.03 / Visible Human Skull and Eyes / CC BY 4.0
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The cutaway window is illustrative; it is not a surgical incision or a
patient-specific craniotomy. Eye alignment is an approximate cross-source fit
for orientation only. Neither asset is a patient scan or suitable for
diagnosis, treatment planning, navigation, device sizing, procedural rehearsal,
or outcome prediction. Specialist review remains pending.

Project asset manifest: vision_pro_stroke_kit_v2 /
asset_manifest_head_details_v2.json, version 0.3.0-head-detail-expansion.

The project-authored Circle-of-Willis overlay is conceptual teaching geometry.
Its coral/gold route lines, arrows, brightness, and spacing are qualitative—not
CFD, a patient measurement, or evidence of flow speed, pressure, perfusion,
collateral circulation, treatment response, or outcome.

Audio provenance gate
---------------------

`FlowBed.wav` and `PressureBed.wav` are locally bundled prototype ambience
resources. Their original authorship and redistribution terms have not yet
been recorded in a source manifest. They are therefore optional, muted by
default, and must not be represented as cleared for public release,
redistribution, commercial use, or App Store submission until their provenance
is independently documented. No third-party audio was added in this pass.

The CT and MRI teaching-reference images are cropped from Figure 2 of
Kaffenberger T, et al., "Stroke population-specific neuroanatomical CT-MRI
brain atlas," Neuroradiology 64 (2022), DOI 10.1007/s00234-021-02875-9,
PMCID PMC9271109, distributed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
International: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The images are de-identified research templates, cropped into modality-specific
panels. They are generic educational references—not a patient scan, finding,
diagnostic result, measurement, or treatment-planning image.
