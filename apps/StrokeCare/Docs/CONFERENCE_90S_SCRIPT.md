# Stroke Care — 90-second conference script

Launch: `--hackathon-demo`  
Promise: **See the pressure. Understand the purpose.**

| Time | Act | Presenter action | Rehearsable cue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–8s | Prelude | Let both calm lines appear, then choose **Doctor → family**. | “When time is urgent, clarity matters. One shared picture can reduce uncertainty.” |
| 8–22s | Case threshold | Turn left, carry **File 78** to the centre, and let the case constellation unfold. | “This is one fictional teaching case—not a scan, record, or recommendation.” |
| 22–30s | Enter | Select **Begin family view**. The complete case room disappears as the brain enters primary focus. | “Now we enter only the part we need to explain.” |
| 30–48s | Orient | Choose **Brain regions** and select one anchored point. | “The points invite one question at a time, while the model stays whole.” |
| 48–65s | Flow | Change to **Blood flow**, select the blockage, and follow the calm direction chevrons. | “Blood approaches, branches, meets a blockage, and the flow beyond it changes.” |
| 65–78s | Permission | Advance, then ask before changing protective layers. | “May I show a non-graphic view of why the team may discuss making room?” |
| 78–87s | Purpose | Fade one layer and show the reversible offset. | “Making room can ease pressure. It cannot undo the original injury.” |
| 87–90s | Close | Advance to the closing reflection. | “You do not have to hold every answer at once. Your care team will guide the next step.” |

If the wearer chooses **Not now**, pause without revealing the final act. If the
wearer pauses, state one idea and ask what remains unclear. No gaze, voice,
camera, behavior, or biometric signal is used to infer anxiety.

Optional system narration reads only the versioned family copy. It is not an
AI medical answer. The local ambient bed can be muted, and no interaction
advances automatically.

Device-language boundary: installation and launch on XCAT are engineering
evidence. Comfort, understanding, spatial-audio perception, and clinical
acceptance require separate wearer and specialist review.
