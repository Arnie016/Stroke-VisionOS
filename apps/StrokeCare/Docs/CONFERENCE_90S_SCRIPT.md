# Stroke Care — 90-second conference script

Launch: `--hackathon-demo`  
Promise: **See the pressure. Understand the purpose.**

| Time | Act | Presenter action | Rehearsable cue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–15s | Threshold | Choose **Begin with the whole brain**. | “This is one fictional teaching scene—not a scan or recommendation.” |
| 15–35s | Orient | Keep **Patient** selected. Indicate the cortex and vessels. | “First we make a shared map: the brain rests inside a fixed skull and vessels bring blood.” |
| 35–58s | Pressure | Continue, then tap the occlusion if useful. | “A blocked vessel can injure tissue. Swelling is a second problem because the skull does not expand.” |
| 58–72s | Permission | Choose **Explain making space**, then ask before revealing. | “May I show a non-graphic view of why the team sometimes needs more room?” |
| 72–86s | Make space | Reveal the bone flap and dural expansion. | “Making space may reduce pressure. It does not restore tissue already injured, and it does not promise an outcome.” |
| 86–90s | Presenter | Switch briefly to **Presenter**. | “The clinician controls every act, sees what is visible, and gets a safety boundary—not a script.” |

If the wearer chooses **Not now**, pause without revealing the final act. If the
wearer pauses, state one idea and ask what remains unclear. No gaze, voice,
camera, behavior, or biometric signal is used to infer anxiety.

Device-language boundary: installation and launch on XCAT are engineering
evidence. Comfort, understanding, spatial-audio perception, and clinical
acceptance require separate wearer and specialist review.
