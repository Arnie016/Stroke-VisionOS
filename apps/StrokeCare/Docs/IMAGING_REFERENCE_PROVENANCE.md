# CT and MRI teaching-reference provenance

The bundled `StrokeCTTemplate` and `StrokeMRITemplate` images are cropped from
Figure 2 of:

> Kaffenberger T, et al. Stroke population-specific neuroanatomical CT-MRI
> brain atlas. *Neuroradiology*. 2022;64:1557–1567.
> DOI: 10.1007/s00234-021-02875-9. PMCID: PMC9271109.

Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9271109/

License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Modifications: Figure 2 was extracted from the openly licensed article PDF and
cropped into separate CT-template and MRI-template panels for use as compact
teaching references. No clinical annotations, findings, or patient identifiers
were added.

These images are de-identified research templates. In Stroke Care they are
presented only as generic educational references. They are not the current
wearer's or fictional case's scan, not a diagnostic result, and not suitable
for treatment planning, navigation, measurement, or outcome prediction.

## Compact modality index

The clinician-facing **Imaging** lane is a teaching index, not an imaging
order set. Its named references have deliberately different evidence and asset
boundaries:

| Visible reference | What appears in Stroke Care | Source note | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CT (X-ray) | The bundled de-identified CT atlas panel above | Kaffenberger et al. (2022), CC BY 4.0 | A generic atlas image, never a case result. |
| MRI | The bundled de-identified MRI atlas panel above | Kaffenberger et al. (2022), CC BY 4.0 | A generic atlas image, never a case result. |
| CT angiography (CTA) | An authored vascular schematic | [ACR Appropriateness Criteria: Cerebrovascular Diseases–Stroke](https://acsearch.acr.org/docs/3149012/Narrative/) | Explains the term only. It does not choose a test, find an occlusion, or suggest care. |
| MR angiography (MRA) | An authored vascular schematic | [ACR Appropriateness Criteria: Cerebrovascular Diseases–Stroke](https://acsearch.acr.org/docs/3149012/Narrative/) | Explains the term only. It does not choose a test, find an occlusion, or suggest care. |
| PET overview | An authored functional-imaging schematic | [NIH NIBIB: Nuclear Medicine](https://www.nibib.nih.gov/science-education/science-topics/nuclear-medicine) | A separate nuclear-medicine concept, not an emergency-stroke study choice in this prototype. |

Near-infrared/optical imaging and proton CT are intentionally **not** shown as
peer options in this core stroke selector. Optical techniques and proton CT
need dedicated, reviewed teaching assets and a separate use-case boundary;
proton CT remains pre-clinical rather than routine diagnostic imaging according
to [Johnson (2024)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38657632/). This avoids
making experimental or context-specific technology look like an automatic
clinical choice.
