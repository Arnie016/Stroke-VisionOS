# Stroke Care — Product Intent V2

## Promise

Stroke Care is a wonder-first spatial neuroanatomy exhibit with two explicit
entry lanes:

- **Curious Learner** — independent, calm discovery through generic teaching
  anatomy, spatial references, optional narration, and questions.
- **Doctor Presenter** — a clinician-controlled explanation using a fictional
  teaching case, a revisitable timeline, and reviewed reference material.

The shared 3D anatomy is educational. It is not a patient scan, diagnosis,
prognosis, treatment recommendation, operative plan, or surgical trainer.

## Story Grammar

1. **Wonder** — a short, skippable spatial prelude introduces the brain as a
   living network rather than a black onboarding form.
2. **Choose intent** — the wearer chooses Curious Learner or Doctor Presenter.
3. **Discover** — quiet anatomy-attached points invite gaze and pinch. A point
   may open one full 3D teaching reference, not a permanent label cloud.
4. **Go deeper** — a Curious Learner may explicitly opt into one more narrated
   layer; the app never assumes consent or listens continuously.
5. **Cross the threshold** — room-scale magnification may reveal a separate
   `Enter the brain` journey with a clear return path.

## Spatial Surfaces

- **Center:** the shared registered anatomy and active lesson.
- **Left:** plain-language explanation and learner questions.
- **Top:** a revisitable teaching timeline.
- **Right:** a curved reference index for anatomy, imaging, interventions,
  medicines, evidence, and generic teaching-model requests.
- **Hand-adjacent:** only tools with a working gaze/pinch fallback.

## Voice Contract

- Curious Learner only; Doctor Presenter remains silent.
- Explicitly enabled, pauseable, and scoped to selected authored teaching copy.
- A proactive prompt asks whether the learner wants more before speaking.
- No ambient microphone, face analysis, inferred anxiety, diagnosis, or hidden
  recording. Missing proxy configuration is shown honestly and remains silent.

## Generic Teaching-Model Print Contract

The app may prepare a **local request for review** for an already reviewed,
licensed, generic teaching model. It must show asset provenance and readiness.
It does not upload a patient scan, place an order, quote a price, promise
manufacturability, or arrange shipping. Those actions require separate
licensing, geometry, privacy, manufacturing, account, payment, and human gates.

## Proof Ladder

Contract checks, a successful build, a Simulator screenshot, a physical-device
launch, wearer interaction, clinician review, and public release are independent
evidence rungs. Current development is Simulator-first; no Simulator artifact is
wearer, clinical, manufacturing, or App Store proof.
